About Maximus Development LLC

Maximus Development LLC is a Texas limited liability company based in McKinney, Texas. We build iOS apps, both our own and for clients.

Principles

iPhone first

We design for iPhone and iPad from the start and follow Apple's conventions, so apps feel familiar.

Accessible to everyone

We support VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and clear contrast so more people can use what we build.

Clear scope and honest timelines

We agree on what will be built before we start, and we tell you plainly how long it will take.
